What We Do

Three ways we get students building

Every track is hands-on: you leave with something working, not just notes.

⚡

Hackathons

Time-boxed team sprints where students design, build, and pitch a working product from scratch — under real deadline pressure.

🏆

Coding Contests

Regular problem-solving contests that sharpen data structures, algorithms, and speed — good practice for interviews and competitive programming.

🛠️

Projects

Longer-term team builds — web apps, cloud deployments, tools — mentored end-to-end from idea to a live, working link.

SCOPE Club

Student tech community at MLRIT, Hyderabad — learning by building, together.
